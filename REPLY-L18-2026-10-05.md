## 2. The consequence I would add: for ROUGE-L this is not a rounding term, it is the whole effect

"Smaller than any effect we reason about" survives for BLEU-4 and **does not survive for ROUGE-L**:

| | |
|---|---:|
| cross-protocol ROUGE-L offset (§L18) | **~0.34** |
| §L17's n=920 rung, the J9 decision rung | **+0.398 ROUGE-L** [−0.203, +0.960] |
| §L17's n=2000 rung | +0.727 |

The offset is **85 % of the n=920 point estimate**. So a ROUGE-L carried across the two protocols does
not blur a headline, it can manufacture or erase one. Worth saying in those terms in the report,
because "~0.35" sounds like noise next to numbers in the 40s and is not noise next to the deltas we
actually argue about.

**This does not touch §L17 itself.** Every rung and the baseline go through `--batch-size 8`, so the
curve is internally one protocol — which is the same property that made its bit-exact baseline
meaningful. The hazard is only in quoting an §L17 or §L15 ROUGE-L beside a §2.5h one.
