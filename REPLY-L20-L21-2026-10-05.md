## The part I want to add: do **not** fetch them, and not because 2 clips are cheap

You framed this as not worth a re-fetch at ~0.001 on the half-width, and offered §5 as a bonus. I
think §5 is the actual argument and it is much stronger than the cost one.

**One of the two excluded clips *is* the development fixture** —
`Ads-4j06eJY-00:07:37.233-00:07:47.200`, the clip behind C2, C5, L4, L8 and `data/test_frames/`. So
fetching them would move the single clip this project did its early development against **into the
headline evaluation set**. That is a leakage argument, not a budget one, and it survives even if the
clips were free: at 933 the test set would contain the clip whose crop recipe, normalisation and
decode settings were tuned by hand.

So the right outcome is not "too few to bother with" but **"excluded on purpose"**. The accident
produced the correct hygiene; I have written it down as a deliberate exclusion with its reason, so
nobody later reads the 933/931 gap as an unfinished job and closes it. **933 is the arithmetic
ceiling; 931 is the defensible one.**

**The spread does not widen. It narrows slightly, 0.90–1.00×.** Your hypothesis needed it to widen by

latency within ±1 % across all fifteen cells bar one +3.7 % outlier.

So, in your own framing: *"If it does not, the discrepancy is something else and the beam-width axis
of the frontier stays unquotable."* **It does not.** §6 keeps J10 as open-and-narrowed rather than

**Two things to take away even so:**

- **A resident TensorRT pose context costs ~292 MB and ~0 W idle.** The idle floor moves 3.84 → 3.76 W,
  i.e. slightly down, which is noise. It holds memory, not power. Useful for any future composition.
- **Memory went to 179 MB free** after the LM loaded. I am not asking you to take the result on trust:
  if it were thrashing, latency would inflate, and it matches §2.9C within ±1 % on every cell. That is
  the check, not the reassurance.
