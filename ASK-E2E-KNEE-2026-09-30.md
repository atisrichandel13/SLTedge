# The 2×2 is built on a superseded operating point

*Revised 2026-10-03 after reading `jetson/e2e_2x2.sh` and RESULTS.md §5.2. The original version of
this file asked for a single corner at 24 fps; your 2×2 is a better design and that part is
withdrawn. What follows is the one thing it still misses.*

## What's wrong

`jetson/e2e_2x2.sh` runs `{source, 16 fps} × {beam 4, greedy}`. The 16 fps row comes from our
priority-1 ask, which was written before the frontier existed. **That priority is out of date.**

The frontier (`results/frontier.png`, RESULTS.md §L16, committed 2026-09-30) puts the recommended
operating point at **beam 4 @ 24 fps**, not at 16 fps:

| cell | system J | BLEU-4 | Δ vs reference |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| beam 4 @ source | 43.4 | 22.87 | reference |
| **beam 4 @ 24 fps** | **36.1** | **22.80** | **−17 % J for −0.07 BLEU-4, CI [−0.53, +0.39]** |
| beam 4 @ 16 fps | 27.0 | 21.54 | −41 % J for −1.33, CI [−2.00, −0.64] |

24 fps is free within noise. **16 fps is an established accuracy loss** — the CI excludes zero. We
are not recommending 16 fps, so the 2×2 as written measures the residual at one cell we recommend
(source) and one we don't (16 fps), and never at the cell we do.

## The ask

**Add 24 fps as a third rate**, making it 3×2 — or if board time is short, just the one cell
`24 fps × beam 4`. Your `--keep-fps` already supports it, so this is a loop bound, not new code.

Your attribution argument is why this is worth doing rather than just taking the 16 fps point: with
source / 24 / 16 you get three frame counts against the same clip and decoder, which constrains the
per-frame term far better than two, and 24 fps is where we need the answer to be right.

## Dropped from this ask

* **`cpu0_MHz`** — withdrawn, you're right that it needed no work. `power_logger` already samples it
  into `aux_avg` and M1's file carries 866.76 MHz, below the 897 MHz downclock point.
* **The single-corner request** — your 2×2 supersedes it for the reason you gave: one more point
  cannot say which factor is mismodelled.

## Unrelated, still outstanding

**We have never produced a board-measured BLEU.** Every accuracy number we quote is offline on the
Mac at n=976; the only board↔offline bridge is the 30-clip batch-size-1 agreement in §5.1. A ~100-clip
end-to-end board pass at the recommended cell would close that, at roughly 15 minutes of board time.
Lower priority than the 24 fps cell above.
